Tricolore

Two players, one graph. Make every region white.

Rules

Blue moves first. First, players take turns colouring a white vertex with their own colour. Once every vertex is coloured, players take turns flipping one vertex between red and blue. A vertex never goes back to white.

Each region is coloured by (red vertices − blue vertices) around its edge, modulo 3: +1 is red, −1 is blue, 0 is white. White vertices don’t count. The outside of the graph is a region too (it fills the background). If every region is white after your move, you win.
